\subsection{Interaction networks carry target degree; wiring is confined to directed regulation}
\label{sec:info}
% SRC: report/10_09_report.md §6 (job 296788), §4.6 (job 294863);
%      /nfs-stor/yuqi.lei/Therapeutic/results/network_information/<slice>_<network>.csv
\note{Lead with the degree finding, then the one place wiring shows up. Fig.~2: heatmap networks
$\times$ datasets for $C$ (degree), $A_{\mathrm{rel}}$ real$-$rewired, $B_1$ real$-$rewired.}

\paragraph{Degree predicts response magnitude.}
In every curated undirected network and in OmniPath, a drug's target degree predicts the magnitude of
its response ($\rho = 0.12$--$0.34$; strongest on Tahoe), and the association survives removing
per-gene publication counts ($\rho = 0.10$--$0.36$). It is absent where degree is unrelated to study
bias by design (HuRI, a systematic screen) and in CollecTRI.
% Table: C real / C after pub correction / rho(degree, pubs) -- §6 columns 4-6.

\paragraph{Locality is degree.}
The raw excess response at one-hop neighbours of a target (0.16--0.21 SD) is the same on the rewired
graph and vanishes when targets are permuted among compounds (§4.6).
After removing drug-level magnitude, undirected PPI networks retain $+0.00$ to $+0.03$ beyond
rewiring; directed signalling and TF networks retain $+0.03$ to $+0.07$ (6/7--7/7 lines in both L1000
phases): OmniPath $+0.055/+0.036$, CollecTRI $+0.066/+0.031$, combined $+0.063/+0.028$ (P1-24/P2-24).
% SRC numbers: §6 column A_rel.

\paragraph{Neighbouring targets barely make drugs alike.}
Drug pairs whose targets are adjacent respond more alike than on rewired graphs by at most
$+0.014$ in any network and dataset---about a tenth of the shared-target excess ($r = 0.13$--$0.18$);
two hops apart is indistinguishable from rewired.

vectors ($0.458$) and are below a one-hot gene identity ($0.466$; $-0.011$, 95\% CI $[-0.021,-0.003]$).

($+0.023$ $[0.003,0.043]$)---the degree effect.

\note{Positive half of the paper: the information that does move the needle, measured with its own null.

Multiple-kernel models over P2-24 show a strict ordering of drug-side information
(plate-map split, full Pearson): chemistry over the line mean $+0.027$; target identity over permuted
targets $+0.035$ ($+0.045$ ik14 split); Cell Painting morphology $+0.052$ ($+0.088$); the drug's
measured response in other lines $+0.131$ ($+0.126$). The one-hop PPI kernel equals its rewired null
($+0.000$), and a degree-feature kernel adds $+0.002$--$0.004$.
